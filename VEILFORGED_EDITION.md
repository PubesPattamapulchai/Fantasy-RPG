# Emberfall — Veilforged Edition

Veilforged pushes Emberfall toward a modern cinematic party-RPG and dark loot-driven action-RPG feel while keeping the world, art direction, names and systems original.

## Gameplay upgrades
- Four World Tiers: Explorer, Adventurer, Veteran and Nightmare.
- World Tier scales enemy health, attack, armor, elite frequency and rewards.
- Nemesis enemies can spawn with multiple elite traits and improved rewards.
- Timed Parry action with partial and Perfect Parry outcomes.
- Execution action for wounded or heavily staggered enemies.
- Eight equippable Runestones for physical, fire, arcane, radiant, poison, defense, momentum and loot builds.
- Expanded Road Constellation with additional offense, survival, arcana and fortune passives.
- Companion Bond progression through camp conversations, with stronger assists at higher bond ranks.
- Mini-map support and richer character-sheet build information.
- Existing stamina dodge, battlefield surfaces, weapon techniques, affixes, initiative, reactions, companions, boss phases and tactical positioning remain integrated.

## Presentation upgrades
- Darker cinematic title and HUD treatment.
- World Tier presentation and Nightmare styling.
- Dedicated Parry and Execution combat actions.
- Nemesis visual emphasis and stronger combat-state feedback.
- Runestone equipment cards and upgraded build screen.
- Compact minimap and enhanced mobile-responsive battle information.

## Compatibility
- Save data from versions 3–6 is migrated into the version 7 state model.
- GitHub Pages and PWA/offline support remain enabled.
- No Windows executables, installers or scripts are introduced by this upgrade.
