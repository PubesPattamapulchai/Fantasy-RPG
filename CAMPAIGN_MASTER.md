# Campaign Master Edition

A deeper tabletop-fantasy gameplay and graphics pass for Emberfall.

## New gameplay
- Initiative order
- Seven unique class reactions
- Rechargeable companion commands
- Creature damage affinities
- Multi-phase bosses
- Wide Sweep and Dread Hex enemy intents
- Rare relic drops
- Expanded class passives
- Save migration from Adventurer’s Codex v3 to Campaign Master v4

## Visual upgrade
- Battle-map grid treatment
- Miniature-style combat bases
- Initiative tracker
- Phase and affinity badges
- Stronger boss phase glow
- Brass combat ledger styling
- Enhanced parchment/codex panels

All names, mechanics, visuals, and worldbuilding are original to Emberfall.
