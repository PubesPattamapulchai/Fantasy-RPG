# Tabletop Roads Upgrade

This version keeps Emberfall's original three-hour campaign and adds an original tabletop-fantasy layer.

## New gameplay
- Pick one of four permanent companions after choosing a job.
- Six ability scores: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA.
- Random road encounters use d20-style checks against a difficulty class.
- Natural 20 succeeds; natural 1 fails.
- Inspiration can reroll road checks or grant advantage on the next weapon attack.
- Battles now roll initiative.
- Weapon attacks roll against enemy Armor; enemies roll against the hero's Armor Class.
- Four freely switchable combat stances: Balanced, Bold, Warded, Cunning.
- Companion passives affect checks, defense, critical hits, mana, or post-battle healing.

## Graphic/UI upgrade
- Dark campaign-book visual theme with parchment, brass, stone, and rune details.
- Animated d20 display in combat and road encounters.
- Character sheet with ability scores, AC, attack bonus, Inspiration, and check record.
- Compass markings and darker exploration lighting on dangerous maps.
- Stronger boss glow and tabletop-styled battle panels.
- Mobile character-sheet shortcut and responsive overlays.

The systems are original to Emberfall and only take broad inspiration from tabletop fantasy role-playing games.
