# Adventurer’s Codex Edition

A deeper original tabletop-fantasy upgrade for Emberfall.

## New systems
- Branching ability-check encounters with three approaches per scene
- Camp and ration management
- Armor Class equipment progression
- Equippable magic relics, including boss drops
- Companion assists once per battle
- Close / Mid / Far tactical positioning
- Saving throws against dangerous enemy techniques
- Renown and choice statistics

## Visual direction
The interface now leans harder into a campaign-book / battle-map atmosphere with parchment, brass, campfire light, subtle grid lines, stronger dungeon lighting, codex equipment cards, and tabletop encounter layouts. All rules text, names, code, and visuals are original to Emberfall.
