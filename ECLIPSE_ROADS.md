# Eclipse Roads Edition

This edition is an original dark-fantasy action/party-RPG visual and gameplay pass. It is designed to capture the *feel* of modern cinematic party RPGs and dark loot-driven action RPGs without copying another game’s characters, maps, artwork, interface assets, dialogue, or exact rules.

## New combat systems

- **Stamina + Dodge:** every battle begins with 100 Stamina. Dodge costs 35 Stamina, changes range, and makes a Dexterity-based evade check against the next damaging enemy action. Stamina recovers through Guard and during enemy turns.
- **Interactive battlefields:** each biome has a unique once-per-battle object or terrain feature. Examples include a powder cask, ore cart, prism pylon, avalanche shelf, healing spring, storm conductor, and fractured Crown rune.
- **Weapon affixes:** C/B/A/S victory ranks can awaken modifiers on the equipped weapon. A weapon can hold up to three affixes. Magic, rare, and epic affixes improve damage, critical chance, AC, attack rolls, skill damage, Momentum, stagger, or life recovery.
- Existing d20-style attacks, initiative, enemy intent, reactions, companion commands, range, stances, weaknesses/resistances, timed strikes, stagger, Momentum, ultimates, and boss phases remain active.

## Graphics and presentation

- Darker cinematic world grading with procedural vignette and ambient glow.
- Stronger saturation/contrast treatment on the pixel world without changing the lightweight rendering model.
- Perspective-styled tactical battle floor with depth shadows.
- Larger boss silhouettes and stronger Phase II/III glow.
- Camera shake on major impacts, perfect attacks, heavy attacks, boss ultimates, and phase changes.
- New Stamina bar, battlefield card, affix styling, and modern dark-fantasy battle controls.
- Mobile HUD now shows Stamina alongside HP and MP.

## New controls

- `0` — Dodge
- `E` — Use battlefield environment
- Touch users can use the dedicated **DODGE** and **USE ENVIRONMENT** buttons.

## Save compatibility

Save format is now version 5. Compatible version 3 and 4 saves are migrated automatically.
